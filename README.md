## Current Venture

### [GOLE](https://getgole.com)

GOLE is an education technology platform designed to make international university admissions more accessible, transparent, and personalized.

We are building an integrated experience that brings together university discovery, student profiles, admissions requirements, and application management.

Our long-term vision is to develop an intelligent infrastructure that helps students make informed educational decisions and navigate the admissions process with greater clarity and confidence.
